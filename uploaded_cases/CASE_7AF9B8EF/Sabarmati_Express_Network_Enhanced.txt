--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On the morning of February 27, 2002, Coach S-6 of the 9166 Sabarmati Express was 
subjected to a coordinated attack near A-Cabin, just outside Godhra Railway 
Station. A violent crowd trapped the passengers inside using heavy stone pelting, 
and poured approximately 140 liters of petrol into the coach corridor via the 
vestibule section. The petrol was ignited, resulting in 59 fatalities.

2. KEY IDENTIFIED ENTITIES (NODES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[CORE CONSPIRATORS & LOGISTICS - Convicted & Acquitted subjects from SIT probe]
- Haji Bilal: Core coordinator; mobilized the mob and coordinated fuel transport.
- Farooq Bhana: Secretary of the District Congress Committee (at the time); core 
  conspirator who directed the procurement of 140 liters of petrol. 
- Razzak Kurkur: Owner of Aman Guest House; primary logistics hub where fuel 
  was stored the night before (Acquitted due to evidentiary gaps, but central to 
  network timeline).
- Salim Zarda (Paanwala): Platform vendor; initiated the physical altercation 
  with passengers and raised the initial alarm to gather the mob.
- Maulvi Umarji: Local cleric; investigated as the ideological instigator 
  (Acquitted, but serves as a high-centrality node in investigation hypotheses).


[EXECUTION SQUAD - Breach & Arson]
- Hasan Charkha: Part of the core group that cut the vestibule.
- Jabir Binyamin Behera: Admitted (later retracted) to fuel transport and pouring.
- Mehboob Yakub Mitha (alias Hasan): Key participant in fuel transport.
- Rafiq Bhatuk: Core executioner; part of the group that broke into the coach.
- Imran Sheri & Shaukat Lalu: Identified in the mobilization and direct attack.
