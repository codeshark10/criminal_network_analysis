2. Narrative Outline of the Incident (As Filed)
The Sabarmati Express, coming from Ayodhya, departed Godhra Railway Station shortly after 8:00 AM. Immediately
after leaving the platform, the emergency chain was pulled, bringing the train to a halt near the 'A' Cabin.
According to the initial FIR and subsequent judicial briefs, an aggressive mob of local residents gathered around the
stranded train. The crowd began pelting heavy stones at the coaches, particularly targeting coach S-6, preventing
passengers from exiting safely.
The mob forced open the vestibule of coach S-6 and poured a highly inflammable substance (petrol) inside. The
coach was subsequently set ablaze, resulting in an intense fire that quickly engulfed the entire compartment.
3. Casualties and Immediate Impact

4. Judicial Commission & Court Rulings
The investigation initiated by FIR No. 9/2002 transitioned through multiple high-level legal bodies over the subsequent
decades:
• The Nanavati-Mehta Commission: Appointed to probe the incident, the judicial commission concluded in its report
that the burning of the S-6 coach was not an accident but a pre-planned criminal conspiracy.
• Special SIT Court & High Court Verdicts: In March 2011, a special Special Investigation Team (SIT) court
convicted 31 individuals for their involvement in the conspiracy and murder. In October 2017, the Gujarat High Court
reviewed the appeals, upholding the conviction of the 31 accused while commuting 11 death sentences to life
imprisonment.

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On the morning of February 27, 2002, Coach S-6 of the 9166 Sabarmati Express was 
subjected to a coordinated attack near A-Cabin, just outside Godhra Railway 
Station. A violent crowd trapped the passengers inside using heavy stone pelting, 
and poured approximately 140 liters of petrol into the coach corridor via the 
vestibule section. The petrol was ignited, resulting in 59 fatalities.
