| 1. EXECUTIVE CASE SUMMARY
On January 19, 2010, senior Hamas operative Mahmoud al-Mabhouh arrived at Dubai International Airport (DXB) at 14:00
local time. Within five hours of checking into Room 230 at the Al Bustan Rotana Hotel, he was targeted and executed by a
highly sophisticated, 26-member foreign hit squad operating under forged European and Australian identities.
The operation represents a masterclass in counter-intelligence compartmentalization. The perpetrators operated in distinct
sub-units (Airport Trackers, Mall Surveillance, Hotel Execution, Logistics, and Escapes) and maintained strict physical
isolation. No team member placed direct phone calls to another team member within the United Arab Emirates. Instead, the
entire operational network was coordinated remotely via an international telecom hub in Austria.
CRITICAL INVESTIGATIVE BREAKTHROUGH
Standard surveillance and localized call detail records (CDRs) yielded zero connections between suspects on the
ground. The breakthrough was achieved by deploying multi-modal network graph analysis-combining spatial-temporal
CCTV spottings, automated border control logs, international telecom relay analysis, and prepaid financial transaction
clustering.
